# Outreach Tracker Template

Use this to track RedactKit validation outreach during the first weeks of recruiter discovery. Copy the table below into a spreadsheet, Notion board, or Linear list and update entries weekly.

Goal of this tracker: turn loose conversations into honest, comparable signals - pain level, repeat usage, willingness to pay - so we hit (or miss) the kill metrics in `12_DECISION_LOG_AND_KILL_METRICS.md` deliberately, not by accident.

## First-week validation checklist

- [ ] Send 50 targeted DMs.
- [ ] Prioritize 20 recruiters / talent sourcers (primary segment).
- [ ] Book or collect 8 discovery calls or async demos.
- [ ] Get 5 prototype trials with real fake-data text.
- [ ] Track whether 3 people use it more than once.
- [ ] Ask whether 2 people would pay $29 once.

If any of these thresholds is missed by end of week 1, revisit the wedge before iterating on the product. Reference the `Kill metrics` table in `12_DECISION_LOG_AND_KILL_METRICS.md` (project-context pack) before deciding pivot vs. continue.

## Column legend

### Segment shorthand

- **R** - Recruiter / talent sourcer (priority)
- **A** - Agency owner
- **F** - Freelancer (copywriter, designer, consultant)
- **VA** - Virtual assistant
- **CR** - Customer researcher / support ops
- **SF** - Solo founder

### Contact method

- LI DM, LI InMail, X DM, IH DM, FB DM, email, warm intro.

### Reply codes

- **N** - no reply yet
- **I** - interested, wants to see more
- **M** - maybe later (timing/bandwidth)
- **NF** - not a fit (segment or workflow)
- **NS** - no sensitive text use
- **B** - already built or uses their own workflow

### Pain level (1-5)

1. They never paste sensitive text into AI.
2. They do, but don't think about it.
3. They've worried about it but haven't acted.
4. They actively redact manually before pasting.
5. They've been burned, audited, or told no by a client / employer.

### Tested prototype?

- **NT** - not tested
- **OW** - onboarded, watched but didn't try
- **DT** - did a single try
- **RT** - repeated tries

### Paid / committed?

- **N** - no
- **W** - on the waitlist (joined via mailto / list)
- **C** - verbally committed to pay
- **P** - paid

## Workflow

1. Add a row when you send outreach. Fill **Name / Segment / Profile URL / Contact method / Date sent**.
2. On reply, fill **Reply?** and start filling discovery columns from the conversation.
3. If they test the prototype, fill **Tested prototype?** and **Used more than once?** within 7 days of access.
4. Promote to **Paid / committed?** only when they say it explicitly - don't infer from enthusiasm.
5. Review the whole sheet at end of week 1, then end of week 2. Compare against the first-week checklist above and the kill metrics in the project-context pack.

## Discovery prompts (use freely)

- "What kind of text do you usually paste into AI?"
- "What do you do today when that text has names, emails, or client info?"
- "When was the last time that workflow bit you, or you decided not to use AI because of it?"
- "If a tool stripped that text locally in the browser, would that change anything for you?"
- "Would that be worth $29 to you once, or are you better off doing it by hand?"

Capture answers verbatim in **Notes** - these are the most useful signal later, more than the column scores.
