# Growth and Validation Work Area

## Goal

Validate whether users will repeatedly use and pay for a local redaction workflow before using AI tools.

Do not validate abstract interest in privacy. Validate real workflow pain.

## First Segment

Primary:

- Recruiters.
- Talent sourcers.

Secondary:

- Freelance recruiters.
- Agency owners.
- Virtual assistants.
- Consultants.
- Freelancers.
- Solo founders.

## Positioning

Primary wedge:

```text
Local redaction before using AI tools.
```

Recommended recruiter headline:

```text
Sanitize candidate text before pasting it into ChatGPT.
```

General headline:

```text
Sanitize sensitive text before pasting it into AI.
```

Avoid positioning as:

- Compliance software.
- Guaranteed anonymization.
- Enterprise privacy platform.
- Secure AI assistant.
- Private ChatGPT replacement.

## Pricing Assumption

Early access:

- `$29 one-time` for first 50 users.

Later possible Pro:

- `$49 one-time` or `$9/month`, only after repeat usage is proven.

## Outreach Targets

First 50:

- 20 recruiters or talent sourcers.
- 10 freelance recruiters.
- 10 agency owners or marketers.
- 5 virtual assistants.
- 5 solo founders.

## Discovery Questions

Ask users:

1. What AI tools do you use for work?
2. What real text do you paste into them?
3. What sensitive information do you remove first?
4. How do you remove it today?
5. How often does this happen?
6. What happens if you forget to remove something?
7. Would a local redaction tool help?
8. What would make you distrust it?
9. Would you pay $29 once if it worked?
10. Can I watch you try a prototype?

## 14-Day Continue Threshold

Continue if:

- 50 targeted DMs sent.
- 15 replies.
- 8 discovery calls or async demos.
- 5 people try the prototype.
- 3 people use it more than once.
- 2 people pay $29 or commit to pay.

## Kill or Pivot Metrics

- 100 DMs with fewer than 10 replies: change segment or message.
- Fewer than 5 discovery calls from 100 DMs: change segment.
- Fewer than 5 trials from 15 interested people: pain may not be urgent.
- Fewer than 3 repeated users in 14 days: not recurring enough.
- Fewer than 2 paid or committed users by day 14: value may be too weak.
- More than 50% of testers complain corrections take too long: improve detection or reconsider.
- More than 50% refuse web version but accept offline: prioritize offline version.
