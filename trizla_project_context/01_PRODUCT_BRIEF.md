# Product Brief — Trizla

## Product name

Trizla

## Category

Local-first privacy utility / AI workflow safety tool.

## One-line promise

**Sanitize sensitive text before it leaves your machine.**

## Longer promise

Trizla helps freelancers, recruiters, agency owners, virtual assistants, consultants, and solo operators safely prepare real work text before pasting it into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, or other AI tools.

It detects likely sensitive information locally, replaces it with stable placeholders, lets the user review every detection, and restores placeholders after the AI response.

## User problem

People want to use AI for real work, but useful work text often contains sensitive information:

- Client names
- Candidate names
- Customer emails
- Phone numbers
- Company names
- Project names
- Salary values
- Invoice amounts
- Order IDs
- Support tickets
- Interview notes
- Internal context

Today they either:

1. Paste sensitive information into AI tools anyway.
2. Manually find-and-replace sensitive terms.
3. Avoid using AI for useful work.
4. Use vague prompts that reduce output quality.
5. Waste time creating fake placeholders manually.

## Target first buyer

Primary first buyer: **recruiters and talent sourcers**.

Why recruiters first:

- They handle names, emails, phone numbers, resumes, salary info, current employer, interview notes, and candidate summaries.
- They already use AI for rewriting, summarizing, screening, and communication.
- The redaction job is easy to explain.
- The first demo is obvious.
- They can be reached on LinkedIn.

Secondary buyers:

- Freelance copywriters
- Agency owners
- Virtual assistants
- Consultants
- Customer researchers
- Solo founders
- Support operators

## Jobs-to-be-done

### Functional job

Help me safely use AI on real work text without manually removing sensitive details.

### Emotional job

Help me feel in control and less anxious before pasting client/candidate/customer text into AI.

### Social job

Help me look professional and responsible with private information.

## Old workaround

Manual cleanup:

- Copy text into a document.
- Remove names.
- Replace emails.
- Replace companies.
- Replace amounts.
- Check again manually.
- Paste into AI.
- Manually restore values later.

## Why this is painful

- Slow.
- Error-prone.
- Easy to miss sensitive values.
- Annoying enough that people skip it.
- Generic AI tools do not solve it before the paste moment.
- Users need the original values restored after AI output.

## Product wedge

The first wedge is not “AI privacy platform.”

The first wedge is:

**A local redaction and restore tool for people who paste real work text into AI tools.**

## First-use wow moment

The user pastes a real candidate/client/support note and sees Trizla instantly find names, email, phone number, company, money amount, and custom sensitive terms before they paste into AI.

## Core differentiator

Trizla is not another AI wrapper. It does not replace ChatGPT or Claude.

It sits **before** and **after** the AI tool:

- Before: sanitize text.
- After: restore placeholders.

## Positioning

Good positioning:

> The local safety layer before ChatGPT.

Avoid:

- “Compliance software”
- “Guaranteed anonymization”
- “Enterprise privacy platform”
- “Secure AI assistant”
- “Private ChatGPT replacement”

## MVP business model

Start with one-time early access:

- $29 one-time for first 50 users.
- Later $49 one-time or $9/month for Pro.
- Avoid subscription until repeat usage is proven.

## What must be true for this to become a real business

1. Users already paste real work text into AI tools.
2. They feel enough hesitation to want a safety layer.
3. Manual redaction is painful enough to pay for.
4. They use it repeatedly, not just once.
5. The product becomes trusted because it is local-first and reviewable.
6. Saved custom terms and profiles create switching cost.
