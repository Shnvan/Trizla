But the useful text contains private details:

- Candidate names

- Salary values
- Internal notes
- Order IDs
- Project names

So you either paste risky text, manually clean it up, or avoid using AI.

## Solution section

### Redact first. Prompt second. Restore after.

1. Paste your text.
2. Review detected sensitive info.
3. Copy the sanitized version.
4. Paste it into your AI tool.
5. Paste the AI response back.
6. Restore placeholders locally.

## FAQ

### Does Trizla upload my text?

No. The MVP is designed to process text locally in your browser.

### Does it replace ChatGPT or Claude?

No. It prepares text before you use those tools.

### Can it miss sensitive information?

Yes. Trizla can miss information or flag false positives. Always review the redactions.

### Is this legal or compliance software?

No. Trizla is a practical local helper, not a compliance guarantee.

### Why not just manually find-and-replace?

You can. Trizla is for people who do that repeatedly and want a faster, more reviewable workflow.
