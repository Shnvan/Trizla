# Outreach and Validation Plan

## Validation goal

Prove that people do not merely like the idea of privacy. Prove they will use and pay for a local redaction workflow before using AI tools.

## First target segment

Start with recruiters.

## Why recruiters first

Recruiters frequently handle:

- Candidate names
- Emails
- Phone numbers
- Current employers
- Salary expectations
- Resume details
- Interview notes
- Hiring manager summaries

They also have a clear AI use case:

- Summarize candidate profiles
- Rewrite candidate notes
- Draft outreach
- Compare candidate fit
- Prepare hiring manager briefs

## First 50 outreach target mix

- 20 recruiters / talent sourcers
- 10 freelance recruiters
- 10 agency owners / marketers
- 5 virtual assistants
- 5 solo founders

## Where to find targets

Recruiters:

- LinkedIn search
- Posts mentioning AI recruiting
- Recruiting communities
- Talent sourcer profiles

Agency owners:

- LinkedIn
- X/Twitter
- Indie Hackers
- Marketing communities

Virtual assistants:

- LinkedIn
- OnlineJobs.ph
- VA Facebook groups

Solo founders:

- Indie Hackers
- X/Twitter
- Reddit SaaS communities

## Qualification criteria

Message people who likely have:

- AI usage
- Sensitive text
- Manual cleanup workflow
- Solo or small-team decision power

Avoid:

- Big enterprise employees with procurement blockers
- Regulated medical/legal/financial workflows
- People who never mention AI or productivity
- Generic “founder” accounts with no workflow clue

## DM scripts

### Recruiter DM

```text
Hey [Name] — quick question. When you use ChatGPT/Claude for resumes, candidate summaries, or interview notes, do you manually remove names/emails/company details first?

I’m building a small local tool that redacts sensitive candidate info before you paste into AI, then restores the placeholders after the AI response.

Not selling anything yet. I’m trying to learn if this is a real workflow pain for recruiters. Would you be open to a 10-minute look?
```

### Agency owner DM

```text
Hey [Name] — quick founder question. Do you or your team use ChatGPT/Claude on client notes, reports, ad data, or customer feedback?

I’m building RedactKit: a local-first tool that sanitizes sensitive client text before anyone pastes it into AI.

I’m looking for 10 agency people to test whether this saves time or reduces risk. Could I show you a 3-minute demo?
```

### Freelancer/copywriter DM

```text
Hey [Name] — I noticed you work with client content. Do you ever hesitate before pasting client briefs, testimonials, survey responses, or internal notes into ChatGPT?

I’m testing a tiny local tool that removes names, emails, company names, and custom sensitive terms before the text goes into AI.

Would this be useful in your workflow, or is manual cleanup already fine?
```

### Virtual assistant DM

```text
Hi [Name] — do you use AI tools to summarize emails, customer messages, spreadsheets, or client notes?

I’m building a simple tool that redacts names, emails, phone numbers, company names, and custom terms locally before pasting into ChatGPT/Claude.

I’m trying to learn if VAs would actually use this. Would you be open to testing a free early version?
```

### Solo founder DM

```text
Hey [Name] — when you paste customer feedback, support logs, or lead lists into ChatGPT, do you clean out emails/names/company info first?

I’m building a tiny local redaction layer for that exact moment. Paste text, review detected sensitive info, copy sanitized version, then restore placeholders after the AI reply.

Useful or not really a problem for you?
```

## Follow-ups

### Follow-up 1

```text
Just bumping this. The reason I’m asking: I keep seeing people use AI for real work text, but the privacy step is still manual find-and-replace.

Even a “no, I wouldn’t use this” would help.
```

### Follow-up 2

```text
Last message from me. If you already have a good workflow for sanitizing text before AI, I’d actually love to hear what you use.
```

## Discovery call questions

Ask:

1. What AI tools do you use for work?
2. What kind of real text do you paste into them?
3. What information do you remove first?
4. How do you remove it today?
5. How often does this happen?
6. What happens if you forget to remove something?
7. Would a local redaction tool help?
8. What would make you distrust it?
9. Would you pay $29 once if it worked?
10. Can I watch you try a prototype?

## 14-day success threshold

Continue if:

- 50 targeted DMs sent.
- 15 replies.
- 8 discovery calls or async demos.
- 5 people try prototype.
- 3 people use it more than once.
- 2 people pay $29 or commit to pay.
